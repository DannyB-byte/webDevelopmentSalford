A little more about us ↗

The Aurelia feeling

A place to slow down
and come together.

Inspired by the warmth of Italian neighbourhood trattorias, Aurelia was imagined as a place where everyone has a seat at the table. From a relaxed midweek supper to a special celebration, our aim is to make every visit feel personal.

Our approach is modern, but our values are timeless: hospitality, generosity and a genuine love of good food.
