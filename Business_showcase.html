Chemo in Comfort: Oncology Digital Platform Problem.

Cancer patients often have to manage treatment schedules, symptoms, appointments and important information across multiple disconnected systems, while clinical teams have limited visibility between appointments.
My role: I developed the initial product concept, researched the patient journey, defined the MVP requirements and designed the proposed user experience and functionality.
Solution: I designed a digital oncology companion centred around treatment planning, daily symptom monitoring, appointments, alerts and a clinically useful patient-generated report. I also considered future integration with wearable technology and remote monitoring.
Outcome: The project developed from an initial social/community platform into a more focused digital-health proposition with a defined MVP, potential NHS use case and future research/innovation pathway.
Skills and approaches: Business analysis, requirements gathering, UX thinking, problem decomposition, market research, product strategy, digital innovation and iterative prototyping.
