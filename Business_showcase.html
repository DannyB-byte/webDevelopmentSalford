Business Showcase

Here are some examples of my work.

Chemo in Comfort: Oncology Digital Platform.

Problem: Cancer patients often have to manage treatment schedules, symptoms, appointments and important information across multiple disconnected systems, while clinical teams have limited visibility between appointments.
My role: I developed the initial product concept, researched the patient journey, defined the MVP requirements and designed the proposed user experience and functionality.
Solution: I designed a digital oncology companion centred around treatment planning, daily symptom monitoring, appointments, alerts and a clinically useful patient-generated report. I also considered future integration with wearable technology and remote monitoring.
Outcome: The project developed from an initial social/community platform into a more focused digital-health proposition with a defined MVP, potential NHS use case and future research/innovation pathway.
Skills and approaches: Business analysis, requirements gathering, UX thinking, problem decomposition, market research, product strategy, digital innovation and iterative prototyping.

UK Greenhouse Gas Emissions Dashboard

Problem: Understanding changes in UK greenhouse gas emissions over time can be difficult when information is spread across large datasets. Presenting raw figures alone makes it harder to identify trends, compare performance and communicate meaningful findings.
My role: I undertook the research, data analysis and development of a Business Intelligence dashboard examining UK greenhouse gas emissions between 1990 and 2023.
Solution: I analysed historical emissions data and developed a dashboard concept using visual representations of emissions trends, annual reductions, per-capita figures and international comparisons. I used data visualisation to turn numerical information into findings that could be interpreted more easily.
Outcome: The project demonstrated how historical datasets can be transformed into accessible business intelligence, helping users understand environmental performance and identify longer-term trends.
Skills and approaches: Data analysis, Microsoft Power BI, data interpretation, dashboard design, visual communication, research and evidence-based decision-making.

Interactive Restaurant Website

Problem: Restaurants need an effective online presence that allows potential customers to explore menus, understand the business and make enquiries. Poorly structured websites can make information difficult to find and create an inconsistent user experience across devices.
My role: I designed and developed a fictional premium restaurant website as part of a university web development assignment. I was responsible for the website structure, visual presentation, navigation and interactive functionality.
Solution: I developed a responsive website using HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. The design included interactive menu categories, navigation elements and a JavaScript-powered enquiry form using email functionality. Particular attention was given to visual consistency, usability and responsive presentation.
Outcome: The project provided a practical demonstration of front-end web development, translating a business concept into an interactive website while applying core web technologies.
Skills and approaches: HTML5, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web design, user interface design, information architecture, problem-solving, debugging and iterative development.
